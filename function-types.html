Stage 1–3: Intuition & Analogy

Built-in vs. User-Defined Functions & Anatomy

The Factory Swiss Army Knife vs. Custom Workshop Tool: Built-in Functions are like factory tools already attached to your Swiss Army knife (like len() or print()). User-Defined Functions are custom instruments you forge in your workshop with def to solve a specific job.

Plain-English Definition: Built-in Functions are embedded directly into Python and available globally. User-Defined Functions (UDFs) are custom subroutines written by programmers to organize custom logic.

Stage 4–6: Built-in Matrix & 4 Pillars of Anatomy
print(*obj)

Outputs text/data to the console.

len(seq)

Returns total count of elements.

type(obj)

Returns the object data class.

sum(iterable)

Sums numbers in a collection.
